## Choice and paragraph handoffs
P1 expands what counts as structural data before explaining value and exposure. P2 shows progressively stronger privacy measures remain attackable; P3 explains the attack's cross-domain auxiliary information and practicality. P4 fairly preserves the achievements and scaling requirements of named attacks. P5 changes question from whether attacks succeed to why/when/how much under a realistic general model. C1–4 carry substantial new work rather than merely recap a prior solution paragraph. 推断：先积累“实践确实成功”的证据，再把理论不足定位成不能解释这些实践；理论、26数据集、无种子算法及发布含义因此是一条回答链，而非四个任意模块。
