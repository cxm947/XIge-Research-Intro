## Nearby method/evaluation checks

Checked §5.1 (PDF p.9): four scenarios are fully studied on U-Net models, whereas SD3/Flux DiT transfer tests specifically use image inpainting. All baselines optimized on SD1.5, 800 images and ten prompts per image, budget8/255 and100 iterations. Checked §5.3 (PDF pp.11–12) confirms direct transfer specifically on inpainting. Thus “four scenarios and four architectures” must not be expanded into an unverified full cross-product, and “universal” remains a study-framed capability claim.
