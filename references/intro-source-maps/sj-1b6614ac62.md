## Nearby method/evaluation checks

Checked §III-A (PDF p.4): free stage versus unlearning-enabled stage; a single leaving participant per round is the study focus, more complex cases left for future work. Checked §IV setup and A/B (PDF pp.8–9): seven datasets, equal participant allocation without overlap in the default setup; natural forgetting after convergence scarcely changes memory metrics, and unmarked checking metrics are explicitly tested as a rival. Thus broad framework compatibility does not establish every participation/data-overlap scenario.
