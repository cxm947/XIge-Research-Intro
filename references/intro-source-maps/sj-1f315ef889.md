## Paragraph decisions and handoffs
P1 establishes protocol importance, customization, observed harm, then turns an unscalable manual practice into an automated implementation-verification question. P2/P3 establish different constraints: semantic diversity versus inaccessible multiparty workflow. P4 fairly considers two natural alternatives before the solution. P5 gives complete high-level flow, then P6–8 reveal design-specific obstacles and remedies. P9 establishes results and their security meaning. P10/C1–2 compresses completed work. 推断：读者先知道为何系统必须同时建模消息、交互与性质，再获知相应定制技术；结尾不需要重新教会三个技术模块，故只有两个较高层次贡献项。
