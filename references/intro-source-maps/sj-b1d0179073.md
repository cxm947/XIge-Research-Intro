## Ending inventory and scope
All four stages, dataset/platform scope, defense trade-off, mechanistic phenomenon and two proposed causes, intervention, quantitative comparison and practical cases precede contributions. Hence C4 can be one integrated sentence; repeating every metric would add little. Critically, introduction's causal wording is stronger than the contribution's association wording; retain empirical association/cause distinctions. The survey is of sampled public applications and uses developer confirmation or repeated-output/manual-review criteria, not a proof that all deployments leak.
